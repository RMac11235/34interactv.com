AnchorLine

Know when your Caddy configuration changed, and put it back safely.

From $5/month. 7-day free trial, no card.

AnchorLine keeps a history of your Caddy web server configuration, alerts you when the running configuration drifts from what you approved, checks the sites you choose and their certificates, and lets you restore a saved configuration after reviewing the full difference. It saves a recovery point before every restore and reads the result back to confirm it.

Why you'll want it

If you run Caddy web servers and need to know when the configuration changed, AnchorLine is built for that. It keeps a history, alerts you when the running configuration drifts from what you approved, checks the sites you choose and their certificates, and lets you restore a saved configuration after reviewing the full difference. The phone app is view only: nothing on your servers can be changed from the phone.

AnchorLine on your phone

The phone app is view only. It shows each AnchorLine install as green, amber or red with the reasons in plain English: configuration drift, unreachable admin APIs, failing site checks and expiring certificates. Nothing on your servers can be changed from the phone. Your servers open no ports for it, and every report is signed by the server's own key.

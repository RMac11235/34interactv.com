VaultDrill

Backups you have actually tested.

VaultDrill backs up your Windows PC to a USB drive, a folder, a NAS, or a cloud storage account you already have. Your backup is encrypted on your PC before it leaves. On a schedule you set (weekly by default), VaultDrill restores a random sample of your files to a scratch folder, checks each one, removes them, and tells you in plain English whether your backup still restores.

What it does

  1. Back up to storage you own. An external drive, a folder on another PC, a NAS share, or your own Backblaze B2, Amazon S3 or SFTP account. You pay your storage provider directly; we never hold your data.
  2. Restore Drill. A scheduled test restore of a sample of your files, with a dated result.
  3. Browse and restore. Open any saved point in time and put files back where you want them.
  4. No telemetry. No account, no analytics. Diagnostic data is shared only if you choose to send it to support yourself.

VaultDrill for Android

The phone app shows each paired PC as green, amber or red with the reason, shows the last backup and Restore Drill history, and lets you run a Restore Drill, pause or resume a schedule, or read a recent log, each after your fingerprint or phone PIN. None of its actions can delete, overwrite or change your files. It is included with every plan and with the trial.

Support

Email support@34interactv.com with the app name, your device, and what happened. For store purchases, refunds and cancellations are handled by Apple or Google, as described below.

All apps

How do I restore a purchase on a new phone?

Install the app with the same Apple or Google account you bought it with, open Settings in the app, and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel a subscription?

On Google Play: profile picture › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. On iPhone and iPad, once an App Store listing exists: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. Google Play: Google Play refund help. App Store: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Will I lose my progress if I change phones?

Saves are kept on your device, not in an account with us. Purchases come back with Restore purchases, but game progress does not move between devices yet.

How do I change ad privacy choices?

Open the game's Settings › Privacy choices. If the row is greyed out, there are no ad choices to set in your region. See our Privacy Policy · Data deletion.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app; everything it stored on the phone goes with it. Some apps also offer "Delete local data" in Settings. We hold no account data about you unless you emailed us.

Plumbline

What is the difference between Plus and No Ads?

No Ads ($4.99 once) removes ads you did not ask for, forever. Plumbline Plus ($2.99/month or $19.99/year) removes those ads and adds free continues and the monthly content drop. Optional rewarded ads stay available either way.

What is the Daily Tower?

The same tower for everyone, with one try a day.

Do I need an account or a connection?

No. Plumbline has no account and plays offline. Your city stays on your device.

Pegasus: A Call To Arms

What is the difference between Plus and No Ads?

No Ads ($5.99 once) removes ads you did not ask for, forever. Pegasus Plus ($2.99/month or $19.99/year) removes those ads and adds the Plus extras described on the Pegasus page. Optional rewarded ads stay available either way.

What are the difficulty tiers?

Four: Cadet, Pilot, Ace and Legend. Level 1 is the same fight on every tier; the tier's enemy numbers phase in over the next few levels. Every number is shown on the difficulty picker.

Does the tier change what I earn?

Yes. Pay falls as the tier rises: Cadet earns more than Pilot, while Ace and Legend earn less, so the harder tiers take skill and replays to keep up.

Does Plus make the game easier?

No. Plus never changes the difficulty.

Skylease

Does time pass when the app is closed?

No. Time runs only while Skylease is open, so nothing goes wrong while you are away.

Are there ads between levels?

No. Skylease shows no interstitial ads. The only ad is the optional Sponsor visit, once a day, which pays in-game money. With Skylease Plus you get the daily Sponsor bonus with no ad.

Vigil

Why can't I fast longer than 72 hours?

Vigil caps every fast at 72 hours for safety. Talk to your doctor before any longer fast.

What do I get after the trial without Pro?

The fasting timer for fasts up to 16 hours and the safety check stay free. Vigil Pro adds longer fasts up to 72 hours, training plans, electrolytes and supplements, refeed guidance, and full history. The free version shows ads, and Vigil Pro removes them.

Do I need a card for the trial?

No. The 7-day full trial starts on first launch with everything on and no card.

Is Vigil medical advice?

No. Vigil is a wellness tool. Talk to a doctor before fasting, especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition or take medication.

TruckWorth

Where are my plans saved?

On your device. Use the export options to keep a copy or share it with your lender or accountant.

What can I do without the license?

Open the complete, read-only sample plan and check a snapshot of local figures for your city. Planning your own truck and every export need the Business license.

What does the Business license cover?

One owner and one food-truck business, with unlimited plans for that business, for $179.99 one time. Every report shows the owner and business it is licensed to. Planning someone else's business needs their own license.

I got a new phone or reinstalled. How do I get the license back?

Use the same Google account, open the license screen and choose Check and restore, then type the owner and business names you used when you bought it.

VaultDrill

The phone app will not pair with my PC.

Make sure VaultDrill is running on the PC and that your plan covers that PC (Personal covers 1, Household up to 5), then start pairing again from the PC.

Do you hold my backups?

No. VaultDrill backs up to storage you own: an external drive, a folder on another PC, a NAS share, or your own Backblaze B2, Amazon S3 or SFTP account. Your backup is encrypted on your PC before it leaves.

What is a Restore Drill?

On a schedule you set (weekly by default), VaultDrill restores a random sample of your files to a scratch folder, checks each one, removes them, and tells you whether your backup still restores.

Can the phone app delete or change my files?

No. Its actions (run a Restore Drill, pause or resume a schedule, read a log) need your fingerprint or phone PIN, and none of them can delete, overwrite or change your files.

How does monthly billing work?

Monthly plans bill the first 3 months together, then month to month. The plan is bought in VaultDrill on your PC; nothing is sold in the phone app.

AnchorLine

Can I change my server from the phone?

No. The phone app is view only by design. Changes and restores happen in AnchorLine on the server.

Do my servers need an open port for the phone app?

No. Your servers open no ports for it, and every report is signed by the server's own key.

What does the free trial include?

7 days, no card, up to 3 servers, with Solo limits during the trial.

GlydeKeys

How do I turn the keyboard on?

Android: Settings › System (or General management) › Languages & input (or Keyboard list and default) › turn on GlydeKeys, then choose it as the default keyboard. Menu names vary a little by phone.

Android warned that the keyboard can collect what I type. Should I worry?

Android shows that warning for every keyboard. GlydeKeys has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything.

How do I keep my learned words on a new phone?

Back up your learned words to a file you choose, then restore that file on the new phone. You can also import a GlydeKeys 1.x backup.

Known Callers

Because we all hate scam calls.

Free. Calls from numbers not in your contacts go straight to voicemail, so only people you know ring through. Android only. Coming soon. For help, email support@34interactv.com. See the Known Callers privacy policy.

A hidden or private number still rang. Why?

Hidden, private and payphone numbers are never shown to screening apps. Use your Phone app's "block unknown/private numbers" setting for those.

Can blocked callers leave a voicemail?

Yes, if your line has voicemail. Unknown numbers are declined and your carrier sends them to voicemail.

It stopped working on my Samsung phone.

Set the app's battery use to Unrestricted. The app shows you how.

Does Known Callers read my contacts?

No. Android simply does not show it calls from your contacts. It has no internet permission, no account and no ads.

Data deletion

III-IV Interactive, LLC's apps do not create an account with us. There is no account on our servers to delete.

Data on your device

Deleting or uninstalling an app removes what that app stored on the device. Files you chose to export, back up, or save somewhere else stay until you delete them yourself.

Information you sent us

If you emailed us, ask us to delete those emails and any personal information we hold about you. Email privacy@34interactv.com or support@34interactv.com. We will answer within 30 days.

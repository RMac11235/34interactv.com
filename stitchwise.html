Stitchwise

A yarn-shop diagnosis and knitting-rhythm puzzle.

Run a small yarn shop where customers bring problems, not orders. Each customer says some needs out loud and hides one, which you uncover by asking the right question. Then choose the yarn, stitch, and pattern that fix it, and knit it in a rhythm-and-pattern puzzle. Yarns have names, codes, and textures; knit is a V and purl is a bump, so nothing is told by color alone. The game is in English and Spanish.

Why you'll want it

If you like diagnosing a puzzle before you play it, Stitchwise is built for that. Warmth, stretch, and look trade off, and no yarn wins all three. Ask up to three questions, pick the combination, then tap Knit and Purl in time.

What is in it

  1. Diagnosis. Each customer hides one need. Ask the right question, then choose the yarn, stitch, and pattern that fix the problem.
  2. Knitting mini-game. Tap Knit and Purl in the stitch's pattern, in time. Quality depends on your timing, not the frame rate.
  3. Colorblind-friendly labels. Yarns have a name, a two-letter code, and a texture. Knit is drawn as a V and purl as a bump, never by color alone.
  4. English and Spanish. The game ships with both languages.

Android first, then iPhone. Play is offline and single-player. Stitchwise is a working title.

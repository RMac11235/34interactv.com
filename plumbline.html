Plumbline

Drop it on the line. Keep it standing.

Free to play. Optional Plus and No Ads.

Drop each block on the plumb line that hangs from the top of the tower. The top floors lean, the wind gusts, and stone, glass and ice change the rules. A bad drop tips off or topples.

Email me when Plumbline launches

Why you'll want it

If you want a block-stacking game where a bad drop tips or topples, Plumbline is built for that. Drop each block on the plumb line, keep the tower standing, and grow a city from the towers you finish. Towers earn brick, stone, glass and steel that buy new block shapes, the Old Town, Harbor and Heights districts, and upgrades.

What is in it

  1. Three lives per run, a Daily Tower (one attempt a day, the same tower for everyone), a ghost of your best run, goals and a stats screen.
  2. Skyline. Every finished tower becomes a building in a city you grow. Towers earn brick, stone, glass and steel that buy twelve plans: new block shapes, the Old Town, Harbor and Heights districts, and upgrades.
  3. Top out. Finish a tower standing with the TOP OUT button.

Price

Free to play with ads. Plumbline Plus is $2.99/month or $19.99/year (no ads you did not ask for, free continues, and the monthly content drop). No Ads is $4.99 once and removes ads you did not ask for. Optional rewarded ads stay available either way.

For 64-bit Android phones and tablets.

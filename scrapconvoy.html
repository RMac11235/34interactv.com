Scrap Convoy

Walk the last supply truck across zombie country. You aim every shot.

You escort the truck stretch by stretch. Read the scout report, pick two ammo types in the workshop, and spend scrap on the gun or the truck. Then walk the truck bed, aim, fire, and patch the hull between waves.

Why you'll want it

If you like reading the next fight and building to it, Scrap Convoy is built for that. Match Ball, Buckshot, or AP slugs to Shamblers, Runners, Brutes, and Armored, and keep the truck rolling through Farm Road and the Overpass.

What is in it

  1. Scout and workshop. See which zombies are coming, pick two ammo types, and buy upgrades, repairs, and patch kits. Every upgrade shows before and after numbers.
  2. The roll-out. Four waves on a side-on road. Walk the truck bed, aim all around, reload, swap ammo, and patch the truck between waves.
  3. Two stretches. Farm Road and Overpass, with a scavenge stop after the first clear. If the truck goes down, rewind to that stretch's scout report and re-plan.

Android first, then iPhone. Play is offline and single-player, in English or Spanish. Scrap Convoy is a working title.

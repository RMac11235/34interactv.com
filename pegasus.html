Pegasus: A Call To Arms

A vertical space shooter.

Fly 30 story Missions, then take on the endless Campaign: breaks between waves, rising threat, and the pilot's Last Stand.

What is in it

  1. 30 story Missions and an endless Campaign across four difficulty tiers: Cadet, Pilot, Ace and Legend.
  2. Achievements and badges. 30 achievements and 14 squadron patches on a Badge Board.
  3. Hangar. Spend the credits you earn on gear and repairs.
  4. Drawn art throughout: ships, enemies, bosses, backgrounds and the pilot.
  5. Tablets. On a 10-inch tablet held sideways, the game plays in a centred upright column at full height.

Price

Free to play with ads. Pegasus Plus is $2.99/month or $19.99/year. No Ads is $5.99 once and removes ads you did not ask for. Optional rewarded ads stay available either way.

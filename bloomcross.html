Bloomcross

A flower-genetics and bouquet-orders puzzle.

You inherit a flower farm and a roadside shop. Customers want bouquets in colors you do not have yet, so you choose the parents, predict what the seeds will bloom, grow the stems, and build each bouquet within the budget. Every color has a name and a pattern cue for colorblind players. The game is in English and Spanish.

Why you'll want it

If you like planning a few moves ahead, Bloomcross is a puzzle about flower genetics and bouquet orders. Read the orders at the counter, use the greenhouse notebook to compare crosses, plant, advance an in-game day, harvest, pick a ribbon that fits the mood, and deliver.

What is in it

  1. Flower genetics. Predict what two parents will produce, and choose a cross that fits the order, your plots, and the deadline.
  2. Bouquet orders. Assemble the requested mix within the customer's budget, and pick a ribbon that matches the mood.
  3. Colorblind-friendly labels. Every color has a name and a pattern cue, not color alone.
  4. English and Spanish. The game ships with both languages.

Android first, then iPhone. Play is offline and single-player. Bloomcross is a working title.

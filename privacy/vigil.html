Vigil Privacy Policy

Publisher: III-IV Interactive, LLC, 1844 Pointe Place Ave, Dunwoody, GA 30338 · iPhone and Android · Effective November 18, 2026

The short version. Vigil is a fasting timer. It has no account. The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob; Vigil Pro has no ads. Your fasting history, plans, and settings are health-related and are kept on your device. The one exception: if crash reports are turned on, an error report could include a piece of text the app was showing when the error happened. We do not sell your personal information.

Information the app handles

We do not collect your fasting history, plans, or settings, and we never use any of it for advertising. Google AdMob collects device and ad information to serve and measure ads. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Crash reports go to Firebase Crashlytics only if turned on. They carry the technical details of an error, such as the error message and where in the app it happened. We do not add your fasting history to them, but they are not filtered, so an error message could contain text the app was showing at the time.

Advertising

Yes: Google AdMob, with Google's consent form where the law requires it (company policy section 2). The free version shows ads. Vigil Pro has no ads.

Purchases

Vigil Pro subscription, processed by Apple or Google. We never see your card.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the fasting reminders you set
  • Run at startup: to restore your reminders after the phone restarts
  • Internet and network state: to load ads, check your subscription and, if turned on, send crash reports
  • Billing: for the Vigil Pro subscription through Google Play
  • Advertising ID (Android): used by Google AdMob; you can reset or delete it in Android settings
  • Ad services (Android): Google's ad library also declares Android's ad-services permissions (advertising ID, ad attribution, and ad topics); Android lets you limit these in Settings, Privacy, Ads
  • Vibrate: so a reminder can vibrate the phone

Children

Vigil is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email privacy@34interactv.com and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Delete the app to remove everything it stored inside the app on your device. Files you saved or exported elsewhere stay until you delete them. We do not keep an account for you; if you emailed us, ask and we will delete those emails. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port personal information and to opt out of targeted advertising. Email privacy@34interactv.com to make a request. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

More detail

Changes

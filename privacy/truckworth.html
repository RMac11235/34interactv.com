TruckWorth Privacy Policy

Publisher: III-IV Interactive, LLC, 1844 Pointe Place Ave, Dunwoody, GA 30338 · iPhone, iPad, and Android · Effective November 18, 2026

The short version. TruckWorth is a planner for deciding whether to start a food truck. It has no account and no ads. Your plans and numbers stay on your device until you export or share them yourself, or send them with an optional feature you turn on. We do not sell your personal information.

Information the app handles

To tie the license to you, the app asks for the owner's name and the business name. It keeps them on your device and sends Google Play only codes made from them (one-way hashes), which Google stores with the purchase so you can restore it later. These codes are linked to your purchase, so they are not anonymous. We never receive the names or the codes. When you export or share a plan, it goes only where you send it.

Optional features (off unless you turn them on)

Each of these is off until you choose it, with a clear consent step in the app. What you send goes to Google Firebase (Google LLC), which stores it for us in the United States (company policy section 4). We never sell or rent personal data.

  • Partner referrals: if you tap to send your plan to a lender, equipment dealer, or insurer, the app sends your name, email, phone number if you give it, the partner you picked, and a summary of your plan, so that partner can contact you. We may receive a referral fee from the partner; the app tells you this before you send. The partner then handles your information under its own privacy policy.
  • Anonymous benchmarks: if you choose to share, the app sends numbers from your plan (such as monthly revenue and costs) and, if you add it, your US state. It sends no name, email, phone number, or device ID.
  • Email list: if you sign up, the app sends your email address so we can email you.
  • Usage stats and crash logs: if you turn them on, Firebase Analytics records how the app is used, and Firebase Crashlytics sends crash details, with device and app details and an app-instance identifier, so we can improve the app and fix errors.

Each email sign-up, benchmark share, or referral also carries the platform, language, app version, and the time and version of your consent. To leave the email list, or to ask us to delete a referral record, email privacy@34interactv.com.

Advertising

None.

Purchases

Business license (one-time), processed by Apple or Google. We never see your card.

Permissions

  • Billing: for the business license through Google Play
  • Network state: so Google Play Billing can tell whether you are online

Children

TruckWorth is a business tool and is not directed to children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email privacy@34interactv.com and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Delete the app to remove everything it stored inside the app on your device. Files you saved or exported elsewhere stay until you delete them. We do not keep an account for you; if you emailed us, ask and we will delete those emails. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port personal information and to opt out of targeted advertising. Email privacy@34interactv.com to make a request. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

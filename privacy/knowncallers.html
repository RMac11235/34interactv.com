Known Callers Privacy Policy

Publisher: III-IV Interactive, LLC, 1844 Pointe Place Ave, Dunwoody, GA 30338 · Android only · Effective November 18, 2026

The short version. Known Callers sends nothing off your phone. It sees the phone number and time of unknown incoming calls, keeps them only in a private list on your phone, and never transmits, shares, or sells them. We do not sell your personal information.

Information the app handles

We receive no data from the app. The app has no internet permission, no account, no analytics, and no crash reporting. It does not read your contacts, call history, text messages, or location; Android does not show it calls from your saved contacts. When it sends an unknown call to voicemail, it saves that number and the time in a private list on your phone (the newest 100) so you can see what it did, plus one on/off setting. The list is excluded from Android cloud backup and phone-to-phone transfer. Clear it any time with "Clear this list" in the app; uninstalling removes everything.

Advertising

None.

Purchases

None. The app is free.

Permissions

  • Call-screening role: Android grants this only when you confirm it, and you can take it away in Settings. The app asks for no Android permissions; its install file lists one internal permission that Android's support library adds so only the app can talk to its own parts.

Children

Known Callers is not directed to children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email privacy@34interactv.com and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Delete the app to remove everything it stored on your device. We hold no account data; if you emailed us, ask and we will delete those emails. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port personal information and to opt out of targeted advertising. Email privacy@34interactv.com to make a request. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

AnchorLine Privacy Policy

Publisher: III-IV Interactive, LLC, 1844 Pointe Place Ave, Dunwoody, GA 30338 · Android · Effective November 18, 2026

The short version. The AnchorLine phone app reaches your AnchorLine server through a relay service, named in the pairing link, that passes messages between them. Status reports and commands are signed, and the receiving side checks the signature, so a change the relay makes to one of them is detected. Other relay replies are not signed. Signing does not hide anything from the relay: the relay can read the messages and records it stores. We do not sell your personal information.

Information the app handles

The relay stores what it needs to deliver messages: your phone's ID, public key, and name, the pairing records, and the server status, alerts, logs, and the commands you send. Past status reports are deleted after 30 days, but the relay keeps the most recent status report for each server; the 30-day cleanup does not remove it. Unpairing the phone stops the relay from passing its messages; the relay keeps a record that the pairing was revoked. Fingerprint or PIN checks are done by your phone's operating system; we never receive biometric data. Crash reports go to Firebase Crashlytics only if turned on.

Advertising

None.

Purchases

Plans are bought for your AnchorLine server.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to reach the relay
  • Camera: only to scan the pairing QR code; no photos are saved or sent
  • Biometrics: to unlock the app with your fingerprint or face; the check happens in Android and we never receive it

Children

AnchorLine is not directed to children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email privacy@34interactv.com and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Delete the app to remove everything it stored inside the app on your device. Files you saved or exported elsewhere stay until you delete them. To have the relay's records about your phone deleted, email the privacy address below. We do not keep an account for you; if you emailed us, ask and we will delete those emails. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port personal information and to opt out of targeted advertising. Email privacy@34interactv.com to make a request. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

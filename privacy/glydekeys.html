GlydeKeys Privacy Policy

Publisher: III-IV Interactive, LLC, 1844 Pointe Place Ave, Dunwoody, GA 30338 · Android · Effective November 18, 2026

The short version. GlydeKeys is a swipe keyboard. It learns your swipe trails on the phone. What you type stays on your device and is never sent to us or anyone else. The one exception is a dictionary backup you choose to save: Settings, Back up writes your learned words to the file and place you pick, and that place's own rules then apply. We do not sell your personal information.

Information the app handles

We receive no data from the app. GlydeKeys has no internet permission. Uninstalling removes everything the app keeps, but not a backup file you saved somewhere else; delete that yourself.

Advertising

None.

Purchases

Paid once in the store.

Permissions

  • Vibrate: for key-press feedback
  • Install updates you pick: only for Settings, Check for updates: it hands an update file you chose to Android's installer, which asks you before installing. It gives no network or data access.

Children

GlydeKeys is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email privacy@34interactv.com and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Delete the app to remove everything it stored inside the app on your device. Files you saved or exported elsewhere stay until you delete them. We do not keep an account for you; if you emailed us, ask and we will delete those emails. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port personal information and to opt out of targeted advertising. Email privacy@34interactv.com to make a request. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Plumbline Privacy Policy

Publisher: III-IV Interactive, LLC, 1844 Pointe Place Ave, Dunwoody, GA 30338 · iPhone, iPad, and Android · Effective November 18, 2026

The short version. Plumbline is a free tower-building game with ads and optional purchases. It has no account. Your city, scores, coins, and settings stay on your device. We do not sell your personal information.

Information the app handles

We receive none of your game data. Google AdMob collects device and ad information to serve and measure ads. Crash reports through Firebase Crashlytics only if turned on.

Advertising

Yes: Google AdMob, with Google's consent form where the law requires it (company policy section 2). Rewarded ads are optional. No Ads or Plus turns off ads you did not ask for.

Purchases

Plumbline Plus subscription ($2.99 a month or $19.99 a year) and No Ads ($4.99 once), processed by Apple or Google. We never see your card.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: to load ads and check purchases
  • Vibrate: for haptic feedback in the game
  • Advertising ID (Android): used by Google AdMob; you can reset or delete it in Android settings
  • Billing: to process purchases through Google Play
  • Ad services (Android): Google's ad library also declares Android's ad-services permissions (advertising ID, ad attribution, and ad topics); Android lets you limit these in Settings, Privacy, Ads

Children

Plumbline is rated for everyone but is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email privacy@34interactv.com and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

Delete the app to remove everything it stored inside the app on your device. Files you saved or exported elsewhere stay until you delete them. We do not keep an account for you; if you emailed us, ask and we will delete those emails. Depending on where you live you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port personal information and to opt out of targeted advertising. Email privacy@34interactv.com to make a request. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

More detail

The III-IV Interactive privacy policy covers every app we publish, including how Google AdMob, Apple, Google Play, and crash reporting work. This page and that one are read together.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

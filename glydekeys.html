Why you'll want it

If you want to glide-type on Android without a keyboard that can go online, GlydeKeys is built for that. It has no internet permission, so nothing you type can leave your phone through it. It learns your words on the phone itself, and you can back them up to a file you choose — including a GlydeKeys 1.x backup — and restore them on this or a new phone.

What it does

  1. Glide typing. Slide through the letters of a word and lift at the last one.
  2. Suggestions and corrections. The suggestion bar shows what space will type. Press backspace right after a correction to get your own word back, and GlydeKeys learns it.
  3. Your words, your file. Back up your learned words to a file you choose, and restore them on this or a new phone. You can also import a GlydeKeys 1.x backup.
  4. The usual keyboard tools. Long-press for symbols and accents, slide on the space bar to move the cursor, emoji, themes and dark mode, keyboard height, vibration and a number row.

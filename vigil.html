Why you'll want it

If you fast alongside training and want guidance that starts with safety, Vigil is built for that. It begins with a safety check, then gives plain guidance for every hour of a fast, a training-week plan, a Water tab and a refeed plan. Vigil ends every fast at 72 hours.

What it does

  1. Safety first. A safety check before you fast.
  2. A hard 3-day maximum. Vigil ends every fast at 72 hours, notifies you, and tells you to break the fast gently with the refeed plan and to consult a doctor before any longer fast.
  3. Hour-by-hour guidance and reminders, including a reminder when you reach your target.
  4. Water tab. A daily ring, sodium, potassium and magnesium bars, a caffeine total and today's timeline.
  5. Training and supplements. A training-week plan with up to two sessions a day, and vitamins and supplements with timing notes.

Price

A 7-day full trial from first launch, no card. After the trial, the fasting timer for fasts up to 16 hours and the safety check stay free, and the free version is ad-supported. Vigil Pro is $5.99/month or $39.99/year and adds longer fasts up to the 72-hour cap, training plans, electrolytes and supplements, refeed guidance, and full history. Vigil Pro has no ads. The safety check and the 72-hour cap apply to everyone.

Vigil is a wellness tool, not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before fasting, especially if you are pregnant, have a medical condition or take medication. Requires Android 8.0 or newer.

Skylease

Build a tower. Run it. It's 1984.

An original vertical tower-management sim. Build offices, condos, shops, a hotel and 19 kinds of amenities floor by floor, lay stairs and elevators, and keep your tenants happy.

What is in it

  1. Amenities: cafe, restaurant, shopping and video arcades, cinema, rooftop bar, health club, clinic, daycare, security, maintenance, housekeeping, parking, conference center, sky lobby and helipad.
  2. Elevators that matter. Upgrade with faster motors, bigger cars, express runs, smart dispatch and wider shafts, and see the measured waits before and after.
  3. Living tenants. Workers keep daily schedules and get stressed when they wait.
  4. Events. Fire, power cuts, breakdowns, VIP visits, grand openings, a market boom or Black Monday, and holidays, each turning out according to what you built.
  5. Stars. Earn 1 to 3 stars to unlock it all. A neon synthwave look, beige computers and synth sound cues.

Time runs only while the app is open. For 64-bit Android phones and tablets.

Price

Free to play with ads, including an optional daily sponsor ad that pays in-game money. Skylease Plus is $2.99/month or $19.99/year.

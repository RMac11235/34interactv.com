Skylease

Build a tower. Run it. It's 1984.

Free to play. Optional Plus.

An original vertical tower-management sim. Build floor by floor from 22 kinds of rooms (offices, condos, hotel rooms, shops, restaurants and more), lay stairs and elevators, and keep your tenants happy.

Why you'll want it

If you want a tower-management sim where elevators matter and tenants keep daily schedules, Skylease is built for that. It is set in 1984. Build floor by floor from 22 kinds of rooms, lay stairs and elevators, and keep your tenants happy. Events such as fire, power cuts, VIP visits, a market boom or Black Monday turn out according to what you built. Time runs only while the app is open.

What is in it

  1. Amenities: fast food, cafe, restaurant, shopping and video arcades, cinema, rooftop bar, health club, clinic, daycare, security, maintenance, housekeeping, parking, conference center, sky lobby and helipad.
  2. Elevators that matter. Upgrade with faster motors, bigger cars, express runs, smart dispatch and wider shafts, and see the measured waits before and after.
  3. Living tenants. Workers keep daily schedules and get stressed when they wait.
  4. Events. Fire, power cuts, breakdowns, VIP visits, grand openings, a market boom or Black Monday, and holidays, each turning out according to what you built.
  5. Stars. Earn 1 to 3 stars to unlock it all. A neon synthwave look, beige computers and synth sound cues.

Time runs only while the app is open. For 64-bit Android phones and tablets.

Price

Free to play. The only ad is an optional sponsor visit, once a day, that pays in-game money; there are no interstitial ads. Skylease Plus is $2.99/month or $19.99/year and gives you the daily sponsor bonus with no ad.

Known Callers

Free. Calls from numbers not in your contacts go straight to voicemail, so only people you know ring through.

One switch: unknown numbers are declined before the phone rings. Saved contacts ring as usual; the app never reads your contacts. A private list on the phone shows what was sent to voicemail. No internet permission, no account, no ads, and no purchases.

Price

Free. No ads, no subscription and no in-app purchases.

Showfield

Read each animal, plan the week, and peak on show day.

You run a small ranch of ponies and pups, each with its own hidden temperament. Morning cues, ears, and tail tell you what they need. Plan five days of feed, care, and training so the right animal is ready for the county show.

Why you'll want it

If you like reading a character and planning a few days ahead, Showfield is built for that. Learn what each animal loves and dislikes, keep mood and bond up, and train in short rhythm and memory games so they peak when the classes are posted.

What is in it

  1. Reading. Each animal hides a temperament. Morning cues and body language give it away, and a notebook helps you name it.
  2. Care and training. Feed, a daily activity, a real petting stroke, and a short training game: gait rhythm for ponies, an agility pattern for pups.
  3. The county show. On day 6, enter posted classes. Ribbons depend on skill, mood, bond, and turnout.

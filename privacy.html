Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026. This policy covers the website 34interactv.com and the apps and games made by III-IV Interactive, LLC ("we"): VaultDrill, GlydeKeys, AnchorLine, TruckWorth, Vigil, Plumbline, Pegasus: A Call To Arms and Skylease.

The short version

Our apps are built to keep your data on devices you control. We do not sell personal data. VaultDrill, GlydeKeys, AnchorLine, TruckWorth and Vigil contain no advertising SDK. Our games Plumbline, Pegasus and Skylease show ads from Google, described below.

This website

This is a static website with no cookies, analytics or trackers. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, keeps standard request logs (such as IP address, time and page requested) under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

Email to us

If you email us, we keep your message and address so we can answer you and fix problems. Ask us and we will delete them.

GlydeKeys

GlydeKeys (version 2.x) has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything you type over the internet. It also has no microphone permission. The words it learns, your settings and your practice text are stored in the app's private storage on your phone, outside Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer. Password fields and other fields that ask for privacy turn suggestions and learning off. If you back up your words to a file, that file is an unencrypted copy saved where you choose; keep it private. You can also import a GlydeKeys 1.x backup file you already have. Uninstalling GlydeKeys or clearing its data removes what it stored on the phone but not files you exported. A "Check for updates" option only inspects an installation file you pick on the device; it does not go online.

VaultDrill (Windows)

VaultDrill sends us nothing by default: no usage data, crash reports, file names, file contents or passwords. Your backups go only to the destination you choose (a drive, a folder, a NAS, or your own cloud storage account) and are encrypted on your PC before they leave it. That traffic is between you and your storage provider, under its terms. License checks happen on your PC.

If you turn on Share diagnostic data with support, VaultDrill can produce a short diagnostic report on your screen (version, settings and recent log lines with paths, names and addresses removed). The app never sends it; you decide whether to attach it to an email to us.

AnchorLine (server software)

AnchorLine stores its configuration history, snapshots, backups and logs on the computer where you run it. It connects only to the Caddy admin endpoints, site-check addresses and SSH hosts you configure. Configuration snapshots can contain credentials; they stay in your local data folder unless you export them.

VaultDrill and AnchorLine phone apps

The Android companion apps for VaultDrill and AnchorLine pair with your PC or server using a one-time code. Status reports travel through a relay we operate on Cloudflare Workers. Reports are signed by your server's key. The phone stores its pairings and recent status locally. The relay keeps status history for 30 days.

For AnchorLine, a report carries only counts and alert headlines; AnchorLine's login token, data folder and Caddy configuration never leave the server. For VaultDrill, a report carries backup and Restore Drill status: a green, amber or red health light and short reasons; whether each backup plan is enabled; last backup time and result; last Restore Drill time and outcome (including file and error counts); disk free-space checks; and license or trial state. It does not include file names, file contents or passwords. Camera permission, when present, is only for scanning a pairing code. Fingerprint or PIN checks are done by your phone's operating system; we never receive biometric data. Push notifications and crash reporting are currently turned off; if we turn them on we will update this policy first.

Plumbline, Pegasus: A Call To Arms and Skylease (games)

Your progress, scores and statistics are stored on your phone. There is no account with us, and nothing is uploaded to us.

These games show ads from Google AdMob. Before any ad loads, the game asks for your consent where the law requires it, using Google's consent tool, and you can change your choice under Privacy choices in Settings. Google may collect device identifiers and ad-interaction data under Google's policies. Plus subscriptions and No Ads purchases (where offered) are bought and managed through Google Play.

The games include support for Google Play Games (leaderboards and achievements) and crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics), both switched off in the current store-bound builds. If we turn either on, we will update this policy first.

TruckWorth

TruckWorth has no account and no sign-in, and it has no ads. The plans you build are stored on your phone. Files you export (PDF, Word or Excel) are copies you choose where to save or share. The Business license is bought through Google Play. The app's network use is for Google Play billing; it does not upload your plans to us.

Vigil

Vigil keeps your fasts, plan, safety answers, water log and settings in the app's private storage on your phone. That information is health-related. In the current code it does not leave the phone: there is no Health Connect integration, no analytics SDK, and no upload of fasting or safety data to us. Vigil has no ads. Reminders use local Android notifications.

Vigil requests the internet permission for Google Play billing (Vigil Pro) and for optional Firebase Crashlytics. Crashlytics is included in the project but switched off in the current configuration, and no Firebase project is configured. The only other network use in the current code is opening the phone dialer if you choose to place an emergency call. If we turn crash reporting on, we will update this policy first, and crash reports will not include your fasting data. Vigil Pro, when it launches, will be bought through Google Play.

Payments

Purchases in our Android apps and games go through Google Play under Google’s terms. Purchases of our Windows software, and any checkout on the web, go through Stripe under Stripe's privacy policy. We receive only what we need to issue and support your license, such as your name, email, country, plan and order number.

Children

Our apps and website are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

Email support@34interactv.com to see, correct or delete personal information we hold about you. We will answer within 30 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date. If a change is significant, we will also say so in the affected apps.

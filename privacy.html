Privacy Policy

III-IV Interactive, LLC · Effective November 18, 2026

This policy explains what information our apps and this website handle, why, and the choices you have. It covers every app published by III-IV Interactive, LLC ("we", "us") on the Apple App Store and Google Play. Each app has its own short section below.

The short version. None of our apps asks you to create an account. Game saves and app data are kept on your device, with the exceptions listed in sections 1 and 6 (files you export, crash reports you allow, store license codes, and the relay used by the VaultDrill and AnchorLine phone apps). We do not sell your personal information. Our free games show ads served by Google AdMob, and Google collects some device information to serve and measure those ads. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google; we never see your card.

1. What we collect

We, III-IV Interactive, LLC, do not run accounts, do not run our own analytics, and do not receive your game progress, plans, or settings. That information is stored on your device and is erased when you delete the app or use the app's own "delete local data" option where it has one.

There are exceptions, and the app sections below say which apply to each app. Files you export, share, or back up yourself (for example a GlydeKeys dictionary backup) go where you put them and are not erased when you delete the app. If crash reports are turned on, they can include text the app was showing when an error happened (section 4). For the TruckWorth, BrewWorth, and WashWorth business license, Google Play receives one-way codes made from the owner and business names. The VaultDrill and AnchorLine phone apps pass messages through a relay service, operated by III-IV Interactive, that stores device, pairing, status, and command records (section 6); those records are not on your phone and are not erased when you delete the app.

We receive personal information only when you choose to send it, for example when you email us for support. We use that email only to answer you.

Some apps include services from Apple and Google. Those services may collect information as described in the sections below and in their own privacy policies.

2. Advertising (free games)

Plumbline, Pegasus: A Call To Arms, and Skylease are free and show ads served by Google AdMob. To serve, limit, and measure ads and to prevent ad fraud, Google may collect:

  • Device identifiers, such as the advertising ID on Android or the Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA) on iPhone and iPad (only if you allow tracking when asked);
  • Device and app information, such as model, operating system, language, and app version;
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address;
  • Ad interactions, such as which ads were shown and tapped; and
  • Diagnostics about ad loading.

Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and some US states), the app asks for your consent or offers an opt-out through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time under Settings › Privacy choices in the game. On iPhone and iPad, the app asks before tracking, and you can change that under iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.

Rewarded ads are always optional: you choose to watch one in exchange for an in-game reward. Buying No Ads or a Plus subscription turns off ads you did not ask for.

Google's use of this information is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and in Google's Privacy Policy.

3. Purchases and subscriptions

Purchases in our Android apps and games go through Google Play under Google’s terms. Purchases of our Windows software, and any checkout on the web, go through Stripe under Stripe's privacy policy. We receive only what we need to issue and support your license, such as your name, email, country, plan and order number.

Purchases and subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never receive your payment card or billing address. The store tells the app which products you own so the app can unlock them, and the app keeps that status on your device. Manage or cancel a subscription in your App Store or Google Play account settings.

4. Crash reports

Some apps can send crash reports through Google Firebase Crashlytics so we can find and fix crashes. A crash report contains technical details such as the device model, operating system version, app version, and the state of the app when it crashed, together with an installation identifier. We do not add your name, email, or the content of your plans or saves to it. Crash reports are not filtered, so an error message could contain text the app was showing at the time. If an app has crash reports turned on, its section below says so and its Settings screen shows the status. See Firebase privacy information.

5. Game services and leaderboards

If a game offers Google Play Games or Apple Game Center achievements or leaderboards, you sign in to those services with your Google or Apple account and your scores and achievements are handled under Google's or Apple's privacy policy. Using them is optional; the game works without them.

7. Children

Our apps are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Ads in our games are limited to general-audience content ratings. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email privacy@34interactv.com and we will delete it.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: delete the app, or use its "delete local data" setting where offered. Deleting the app does not delete files you exported or backed up yourself, crash reports already sent, or records held by Apple, Google, or the VaultDrill and AnchorLine relay. Crash reports and store records follow Google's and Apple's own retention and deletion rules. To ask us to delete emails you sent us, or relay records about your phone, email privacy@34interactv.com.
  • Ads: change consent under Settings › Privacy choices in the game; reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings; turn off "Allow Apps to Request to Track" on iPhone and iPad.
  • Your rights: depending on where you live (for example California, other US states, the EEA, or the UK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port personal information, to opt out of "sale" or "sharing" for targeted advertising, and to complain to a regulator. We do not sell personal information. To make a request, email privacy@34interactv.com. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

For a short guide, including how to remove data stored on your device, see Data deletion.

9. Security and retention

Data the apps keep on your device is protected by your device's own security. The VaultDrill and AnchorLine relay, which we operate, deletes past status reports after 30 days; it keeps the most recent status report and its device, pairing, and command records while the service runs. Crash reports are kept under Firebase's retention rules. Connections to Apple, Google, and your own paired servers use encryption in transit. We keep support emails only as long as needed to help you and meet legal obligations.

10. This website

This is a static website with no cookies, analytics or trackers. Our hosting provider, GitHub Pages, keeps standard request logs (such as IP address, time and page requested) under the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

This website uses no cookies, no analytics, and no advertising. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and pages requested) for security and reliability.

App and game pages have optional feedback boards, and game pages have message boards. They are run on GitHub Discussions through giscus. Nothing from GitHub or giscus loads until you click to open a board. If you open one, it is loaded from giscus.app and GitHub. To post or vote, you sign in with your GitHub account. Your posts and votes are public on GitHub under the GitHub General Privacy Statement. To remove a post, delete it on GitHub or email us.

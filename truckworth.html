TruckWorth

A financial plan for your food truck, built from your own numbers.

TruckWorth turns your menu, places, staff and loan into a 3- or 5-year financial model on your Android phone. No account and no sign-in; your plans stay on the phone.

What it does

  1. Financial statements. Profit and loss, cash flow and balance sheet that tie every month; year 1 by month, then by month or quarter.
  2. Loan and startup budget. A loan schedule (interest, principal, remaining balance) and a sources-and-uses startup budget: truck, kitchen, wrap, POS, permits, opening food and cash reserve.
  3. Profit by menu item and by place, plus a staff plan with start months and wages.
  4. Estimates. Estimated income taxes by business type and a business value (DCF) with a plain-words summary, both labeled as estimates.
  5. Dashboard. 20+ charts, key numbers and health checks.
  6. Business plan. A PDF for your lender, a Word file to edit, and the full model as an Excel file.

Price

Business license: $179.99 one-time, bought through Google Play. No ads and no subscription.

Requires Android 7.0 or newer. TruckWorth is a planning tool, not tax, legal or financial advice.
